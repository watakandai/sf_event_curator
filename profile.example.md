# Who this event feed is for

Plain English, no schema. The LLM ranker reads this whole file as-is, so write
it the way you'd brief a friend who's picking things for you. Specifics beat
adjectives: "I'll drive 45 minutes for live music but not for a street fair"
tells the ranker far more than "I like music".

Editing this file changes the profile hash, which re-ranks every event on the
next run. That's intended - but it does mean a reword costs a full re-rank.

## Background

- Where you live, and how far you'll actually travel on a weeknight vs a weekend.
- Whether you have a car, bike, or rely on transit.
- Who you usually go with: alone, partner, friends, kids, a dog.

## What you want more of

- Genres, scenes, topics, activities.
- Scale: intimate venues or big festivals?
- Time of day: early mornings, afternoons, late nights?

## What to push down

- Things you're reliably not interested in.
- Hard constraints: no 21+ if you don't drink, no standing-only shows, etc.

## Budget

- What "expensive" means to you, and whether free matters.
