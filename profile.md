## What to push down

- Live music is not my thing. Gigs, club nights, DJ sets, touring shows and
  bar bands should rank low by default, even when they're well reviewed.
- The exception is the handful of festivals that are destinations in their
  own right rather than just a lineup: Outside Lands, Portola, Hardly
  Strictly Bluegrass, Stern Grove. Those I do want to see.
- Merch pop-ups and brand activations dressed up as events.
