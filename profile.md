# Profile: SF Event Recommender System

Who this event feed is for

Background
- Based in San Francisco, Bay Area generally.
- **Travel Radius & Availability:** Weekends are open for events in SF or regional day trips (up to ~1–1.5 hours driving distance, e.g., North Bay, East Bay, Marin) for top-tier signature events. Weeknights are strictly for high-relevance events within San Francisco proper (accessible after 5:30 PM).

What I want more of
- **Major Signature & Annual Bay Area Traditions:** Large, marquee annual events worth planning around far in advance—the ones you would regret missing (e.g., Mill Valley Fall Arts Festival, Hardly Strictly Bluegrass).
- **Outdoor, Nature & Active Events:** Outdoor gatherings, trail/running events, outdoor film screenings, open-air community gatherings, and nature/park programming.
- **Tech, Robotics & Frontier Tech Meetups:** Industry/community showcases, specialized talks, and gatherings focused on autonomous systems, robotics, AI, and hardware innovation.
- **Food, Cultural & Specialty Markets:** Japanese cultural and food festivals, specialty coffee pop-ups, neighborhood night markets, and unique local culinary showcases.
- **Art & Craft Festivals, Local Street Fairs:** Street fairs and artisan markets with genuine local neighborhood character, independent craft showcases, and local art walks (e.g., Mission Open Studios, Renegade Craft Fair).

What to push down
- **Live Music & Nightlife (Default Low):** I'm not interested infinding new music, following bands, and exploring various bands. Don't get me wrong. I like EDM, techno and popular music at clubs and bars. Please recommend major landmark/destination music festivals that double as cultural institutions (e.g., Outside Lands, Portola, Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, Stern Grove Festival) and popular bands/artists. 

Budget
- Budget is not really a problem. 
- Paid tickets are welcome for high-quality annual destination events, unique cultural festivals, or specialized technical showcases.
